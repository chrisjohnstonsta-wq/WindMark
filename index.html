GPS WITHOUT CELL SERVICE
GPS works without cellular service, but fixes may take longer or be less accurate under heavy tree cover, steep terrain, or limited sky view. WindMark records GPS accuracy and fix age with every observation.

A poor or stale fix never prevents recording an observation — the quality is stored alongside it instead.
